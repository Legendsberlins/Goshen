Feeding Africa. Exporting Her Richness.

Goshen Giant Food Limited is a proudly Nigerian agro-processing company producing a wide range of culturally-rooted and export-compliant food products under the Naturis Giant brand. From dried seafood and native vegetables to plant-milk beverages and all-natural flours, we're committed to quality, sustainability, and food security across Nigeria and beyond.

With advanced facilities in Uyo, Akwa Ibom State, and a distribution network reaching North America, Europe, and West Africa, we bridge tradition with innovation to deliver safe, nutritious, and affordable food to every table.

Why Choose Goshen?

100% Natural Ingredients

We use only naturally grown and responsibly sourced ingredients—free from artificial preservatives, additives, and GMOs. What you see is what you eat.

Proudly African, Globally Trusted

Rooted in Africa, reaching the world. Our products bring the rich taste of African heritage to homes across Nigeria, the diaspora, and beyond.

Reliable Nationwide & International Delivery

We deliver across Nigeria and ship to the USA, UK, Canada, and beyond. Whether retail or wholesale, we get your order to your doorstep—fast and fresh.

Privacy Policy

At Goshen Giant Food Limited, your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website or shop with us online.

Information We Collect

We may collect the following:

  • Personal Identification: Name, Email, Phone Number, Delivery Address
  • Transaction Data: Payment information, order history
  • Technical Data: IP address, browser type, cookies

How We Use Your Information

  • To process and fulfill your orders
  • To communicate with you (e.g., order updates, newsletters)
  • To improve our website and customer experience
  • To comply with regulatory requirements

Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share data with:

  • Payment processors
  • Shipping/logistics providers
  • Regulatory bodies (e.g., NAFDAC, customs)

All third-party partners are bound by data protection laws.

Your Rights

You may request to access, correct, or delete your personal data at any time by contacting us at privacy@goshengiantfoods.com.

Cookies

We use cookies to enhance user experience. You may disable cookies in your browser settings.

Security

We use industry-standard encryption and data protection tools to safeguard your information.
